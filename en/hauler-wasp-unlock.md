## Start near the crashed Cloud Cruiser

**Developer reply:** [Sofia at Apog](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805072991098811/) tells the player trying to lift the core to find another type of Wasp in a cave close to the crashed Cloud Cruiser. The question author later confirms finding the craft and obtaining the Cruiser. This establishes the cave lead, not every switch below.

## Keycard, power and exit doors

**Community route, checked November 3:** thoth describes the following in the [Hauler acquisition thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805391546199210/#c573805391546235310). These steps are a player's account, not our own playtest.

<details>
<summary>Show the cave and hangar sequence (spoilers)</summary>
<ol>
<li>Follow the underground tunnel onward to the room with the keycard.</li>
<li>Backtrack a short way. Near the locked door, stun/zap the fallen wall coil described in the reply.</li>
<li>Use the keycard to open the cave-entry door.</li>
<li>Use the ground-level console to restore power.</li>
<li>Climb the multi-storey stairs to the gantry control room. Operate its switches to open the large hangar doors before flying out.</li>
</ol>
</details>

If you already have the Hauler but cannot leave, the ground console and elevated door-control room are separate checkpoints. A locked keycard door and the large exit doors are not the same interaction.

## If your doors still do not open

The [official EA 1.0-7 notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1798230/view/712290761518876789) list large hangar doors opening inconsistently after save/load. That is a patch-specific defect, separate from missing power or an uncollected keycard. Compare your version and which door is failing; no universal reset is verified here.

## Once outside

Follow the [reactor-core delivery guide](/guides/reactor-core-delivery/). The Hauler magnet is the relevant lifting system, while the Utility Wasp carries your camp. EA 1.0-8 enlarges the magnet's area of effect; it does not establish an exact pickup radius. See [Cargo/Hauler salvage preparation](/guides/cargo-wasp-salvage/) and the [main task navigator](/guides/early-access-walkthrough/).
