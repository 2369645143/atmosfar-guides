## Weather signals before takeoff

The developer describes an unusual buzzing phenomenon: animals take shelter and electrical equipment becomes unreliable. The diary explicitly warns against flying a Wasp during it. Heavy rain, cyclones and lightning are also reasons to wait. No dependable forecast for the buzzing is described. [Weather source](https://www.atmosfar.game/feed/dev-blog-8-the-world-of-atmosfar).

| What you notice | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Buzzing, animals seeking shelter, electrical trouble | Do not start a flight during the phenomenon |
| Heavy rain, cyclones or lightning | Delay departure |
| Fog or an unclear landing area | Editorial advice: regain a clear view before committing |

## Read landmarks rather than guessing a direction

The level-art diary explains that distinctive rocks, trees and ruins help players orient themselves. The later concept-art diary also describes environmental cues that draw attention to possible interactions. A landmark is a reference point, not proof of a safe route or guaranteed loot. [Level-art source](https://www.atmosfar.game/feed/dev-blog-16-breathing-life-into-tycos) · [Environmental cues](https://www.atmosfar.game/feed/dev-blog-17-making-it-make-sense).

### Suggested expedition routine

These are editorial planning steps:

1. Choose a nearby objective and identify a return landmark before leaving.
2. Describe that landmark to teammates using its shape and surrounding structures.
3. At each new viewpoint, look back and confirm that you can still recognize the route.
4. If visibility deteriorates, avoid extending the expedition. If already airborne, assess reachable landing areas using the current conditions; this guide cannot supply a universal emergency maneuver.
5. Before returning to flight, check the weather, fuel and vehicle condition again. No fixed waiting timer guarantees safety.

## Changing altitude is a separate preparation task

The world diary describes stronger engines as a requirement for travel between island layers. More fuel alone does not solve that requirement. Verify the capabilities of your current craft before choosing a destination above or below your familiar area.
