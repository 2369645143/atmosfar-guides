## Use the Hauler, not the starter craft

**Developer guidance:** Sofia at Apog directs players to a different Wasp in the cave near the crashed Cruiser. Obtain the [Hauler and open its hangar exit](/guides/hauler-wasp-unlock/) before attempting the lift. [Original developer reply](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805072991098811/).

## Delivery and installation are separate steps

**Community answer, checked November 3:** hypercool, reply #12 in the same thread, says to drop the core through the top, then return below to the reactor and equip/select the core for use. Another player describes dropping it on a Wasp landing pad, where it disappeared and reappeared at the wreck. The upper intake can be mistaken for a fan.

These checkpoints paraphrase player reports. They do not establish exact keys, coordinates or guaranteed success on every save. Use the on-screen prompts rather than an invented button sequence.

<h2 id="core-recovery">Core stuck or gone: match your patch</h2>

**Official fixes:** EA 1.0-8 covers specific respawn/extraction failures; EA 1.0-9 adds respawn-location and corner-trap fixes. For a core that is already stuck, the developer says some players may need to save and reload to trigger its respawn. [Official notes and full version distinction](/guides/ea-patch-status/).

This is a scoped core-recovery instruction, not a cure for every lost item. EA 1.0-10 adds a core waypoint/recovery feature in the **Experimental preview**; do not assume it is on the default branch. Save experimental progress in a new slot.

## If it still fails

**Editorial report checklist:** record version/branch, current objective, whether the core is attached, where it was released, and whether the reactor accepted it. Keep [0 MN thrust](/guides/cloud-cruiser-first-base/#zero-thrust-unconfirmed) separate: no confirmed universal solution is established for that symptom.
