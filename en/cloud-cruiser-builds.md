## Choose a purpose before extending the hull

**Editorial build plans:** these are ways to organize a ship, not tested blueprints with fixed part counts.

| Plan | Put first | Check before adding more |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First expedition base | Reactor access, storage and an unobstructed Wasp route | Existing systems work and supplies are reachable |
| Salvage ship | Hauler approach and a material-processing area | Receiving opening and unloading path remain clear |
| Co-op home | Shared work area and paths between crew stations | Players can move and work without blocking docking |

## Power, lift and fuel are different budgets

**Developer systems overview:** the [Cloud Cruiser diary](https://www.atmosfar.game/feed/dev-blog-10-cloud-cruiser-101), made with alpha footage, describes weighted hull parts, electrically supported lift and limited reactor output. It also describes built Wasp pads and a material processor for large scrap. Exact costs and output must be read from the current build.

**Official current patch:** EA 1.0-8 adds a warning when fuel supply cannot support requested thrust. A reactor/power problem and fuel-demand warning are different checks; more reactors are not a proven answer to every motion problem. [Patch status](/guides/ea-patch-status/).

## Rebuilding without losing lift

**Community warning:** in the [rebuild discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805391546349135/), Scooby Doo advises placing replacement balloons before removing existing ones, warning that removing lift can make the ship fall. Another player's stripped-down ship is one account, not a universal safe minimum build.

**Editorial sequence:** retain working flight systems, change one area at a time, inspect the displayed budgets, and keep the approach to Wasp pads and cargo intake open. Do not demolish the entire ship to imitate a screenshot. This page does not supply a validated lift formula or a guaranteed optimum layout.

## Construction problems by symptom

- A dismantled Wasp pad cannot be placed again: compare the specific EA 1.0-7 collision fix.
- Materials are in storage but crafting does not see them: compare the EA 1.0-6/1.0-7 inventory changes; Experimental 1.0-10 separately previews co-op client crafting changes.
- A build produces **0 MN**: the [zero-thrust report](/guides/cloud-cruiser-first-base/#zero-thrust-unconfirmed) has no confirmed universal solution.
