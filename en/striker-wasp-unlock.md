## Start with the military base objective

**Developer explanation:** Fredrik confirms that the military base contains the Striker, while weather data is in the largest settlement on the same island. These are different destinations. [Original reply](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805072991227463/#c573805072991229966).

This guide organizes verified answers around the Fort Hannigan blockers. It is not a turn-by-turn route from the beginning of the game; follow your active objective until you reach the relevant task.

## Check the keycard and four-fuse task

**Community answers:** the [keycard thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805391546294705/) points to the room beside the four fuses, with the question author confirming they found it. The [fuse discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805391546180015/) describes one fuse already inserted and three locations, including a hangar that needs power.

The fuse thread also contains missing-fuse reports; this is a community checklist, not guaranteed recovery. [Full source distinctions and conflicting reports](/guides/opening-problems/#fort-hannigan-fuses).

## A door bug is not a missing keycard

**Official patch notes:** EA 1.0-6 lists the Fort Hannigan Striker hangar door/fuse issue (VENU-13528). EA 1.0-7 addresses older-save Fort Hannigan progression/frequency resets and inconsistent large hangar doors. EA 1.0-8 fixes Quinn dialogue ending too early after obtaining the Striker. [Patch helper](/tools/troubleshooting/?symptom=quest).

If the craft is acquired but dialogue stops, record that separately from being locked out of the hangar. No verified crafting recipe or “research everything to unlock the Striker” shortcut is established here.
