## Before your first flight
Identify the Wasp and check takeoff, direction and landing actions before testing bindings somewhere safe. The official opening-system overview uses the Utility Wasp; Scout belongs to the later The Rime update. Do not search for an opening Scout based on the old sequence. See [first-flight preparation](/guides/prologue-walkthrough/) and [Wasp classes](/guides/wasp-classes-guide/).

## Ground interaction and remapping checks
1. Record the build, input device and failing action. Distinguish movement, interaction and a menu capturing input.
2. Compare the assigned binding with the prompt; check whether a chat or text field has focus.
3. If your build provides remapping, change one action at a time, test in a safe place and note whether it persists after returning to the menu or relaunching.
4. Before restoring settings, capture your custom layout. Do not delete settings or save files to test a forum suggestion.

These are diagnostic suggestions, not confirmation of a fixed key, gamepad feature or FOV control. The searchable table below is an action checklist, not a default-key chart.

## Do old Demo remapping reports apply?
In the [March 2026 keyboard discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/806847153386347909/), a developer reply described the Demo as no longer actively maintained and full-game keybinding work as ongoing. That historical reply cannot prove either an EA fix or a continuing EA bug.

For a report include the build, device, action name, binding screenshot and whether relaunching reproduces it. After EA launches, the in-game reporting tool described by the developer is another route.
