## What is the Cloud Cruiser?
The Cloud Cruiser is a flying base that travels with your expedition. Developer material describes onboard work and customization, and makes clear that managing the vessel is a larger task than flying an individual Wasp. Earlier system demonstrations were made in an alpha build.

## Does the demo include it?
The April 2026 development update says the Cloud Cruiser was outside the demo's progression. A demo ending before you obtain the base is not proof that you missed an unlock. This page does not supply an unverified quest name, repair recipe or guaranteed acquisition time.

## A first-base preparation plan
The following is an editorial workflow to apply once your current objectives give access to the vessel:

1. Finish the objective shown by the game and inspect the systems it actually makes available.
2. List the tasks you want to do aboard: storage, processing, preparation for an expedition and maintenance.
3. Keep the supplies for your next task together; check each displayed recipe before spending materials on optional construction.
4. Plan a clear Wasp return and unloading area. Confirm the current interaction prompts rather than relying on a claimed automatic docking shortcut.
5. In co-op, agree who handles travel and who checks supplies. Regroup before moving away from teammates.

If a work area does not function, check its visible requirements and current objective first. Compare your installed patch with the [official updates](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/announcements/) before rebuilding or discarding progress.

## Reactor core missing during loading?

The [EA 1.0-5 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/allnews/), checked October 31, list fixes for core loss after Hauler magnet detachment and a core despawning in the cargo lift. Compare the precise action and your version before assuming every missing-core case has been solved. The patch does not promise restoration of already lost cores.

The [reactor discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805072991231149/) includes both an installation suggestion and reports of failed attempts. This guide does not turn that disputed sequence into a guaranteed route. If the problem continues, record the loading step, current objective and version for the in-game bug reporter. See [the current loss/storage distinctions](/guides/opening-problems/#ea-10-5-reactor-and-camp-storage-fixes).

## Docked Camp Hub trunk interaction

[Developer devl0f confirms that docked Camp Hub trunk access is intended](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805072991228002/#c573805072991228343), checked October 31, 2026. The reported inability to interact is a tracked bug. The developer suggests a large Wasp pad as a **possible** workaround, without guaranteeing success. A proposed experimental hotfix is not confirmation that a fix has shipped.

Keep this separate from boxes failing to place on extreme terrain, manual-attachment despawn and forgetting where a camp was left. If the trunk is visible but cannot be opened after docking, record that symptom and the pad/version; do not treat it as proof that the contents were deleted. See [the storage symptom helper](/tools/troubleshooting/?symptom=storage) for these distinctions.
