Aerial engagements in **ATMOSFAR** represent high-stakes dogfights where flight maneuverability and heat regulation are just as decisive as raw firearm damage. Whether defending the **Cloud Cruiser** against swarming sky drakes or clearing drone nests, mastering weapon parameters ensures mission survival.

---

## 1. Wasp Scout Craft Hardpoints

The agile Wasp scout features two modular wing hardpoints and one nose mount:

### Nose Kinetic Autocannon
* **Damage Type**: Ballistic / Kinetic.
* **Effective Range**: 150m to 400m.
* **Role**: Primary shredder against biological carapaces and unshielded scout drones. Fires standard 20mm caseless rounds manufactured in the machine shop.

### Wing Energy Pulse Repeaters
* **Damage Type**: Thermal / Ionized Energy.
* **Effective Range**: 50m to 250m.
* **Role**: Rapid shield-stripping weapon. Draws capacitor energy directly from the Wasp's battery rather than consuming physical ammunition.

## 2. Cloud Cruiser Point Defense Grid

Your aerial fortress is vulnerable when stationary during mining expeditions. Protect the carrier using its heavy defense grid:

* **Dual Flak CIWS Turrets**: Positioned on the dorsal and ventral hull to create a 360-degree perimeter denying entry to airborne fauna.
* **Manual Overcharge Console**: Crew members can man the dorsal turret directly to target elite armored threats with 200% tracking precision.
* **Concussion Decoy Pods**: Dispensed from the stern to divert homing missiles and confused flock leaders away from the carrier's fragile engine vents.

---

## 3. High-G Dogfight Evasion Tactics

1. **Energy Management in Thin Air**: Continuous laser fire spikes weapon heat. In thin air (>5,000m), weapon cooling takes 40% longer. Fire in disciplined 3-second bursts.
2. **Dive & Zoom Tactics**: When outnumbered, dive toward dense cloud decks. Pull into a vertical climb using afterburner boost (`Space`) to re-engage with altitude and speed advantage.
