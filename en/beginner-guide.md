Surviving your first few hours in **ATMOSFAR** requires a fundamental mental shift from traditional ground-bound survival games. On planet **Tycos**, the ground beneath your feet is not infinite bedrock—it consists of fragmented, floating landmasses suspended thousands of meters above a toxic chemical abyss.

Here are the 10 core survival rules verified for the Early Access launch.

---

## 1. Double-Tap Space for Instant Glider Deployment
When traversing jagged island peaks, never run blindly off edges. Double-tapping `Space` deploys your personal aerofoil glider. Gliding consumes stamina, so always survey your landing zone before committing to a jump.

## 2. Bind the Grappling Hook to Reflex Muscle Memory
If unexpected downdrafts catch your glider, tap `F` to launch your pneumatic grapple hook. Latching onto cliff faces arrests falling velocity instantly and prevents fatal drops into the sub-zero cloud sea.

## 3. Keep an Eye on Barometric Pressure
As you ascend above 2,500 meters, atmospheric pressure drops sharply. Watch your HUD altimeter: once the oxygen gauge flashes amber, your suit consumes backup canisters at 2.5x speed. Keep spare O2 canisters slotted on quick-use keybind `X`.

## 4. Master Wasp VTOL Hovering Before Dogfighting
When piloting the Wasp scout craft, press `V` to engage vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) hover mode. Hovering allows you to stabilize position directly over narrow supply crates or ore nodes without drifting in crosswinds.

## 5. Bank with Aileron Roll (Q / E) in Dives
Rudder yaw alone (`A / D`) turns slowly in thin air. To evade hostile aerial predators or sudden lightning spires, bank with `Q` or `E` while pulling back on the flight stick to execute tight high-G barrel rolls.

## 6. The Cloud Cruiser is Your Unlimited Safe Haven
The central **Cloud Cruiser** aerial fortress houses an active hydroponics bay and reactor core. Returning to the docking pad automatically replenishes personal oxygen, repairs light suit armor damage, and recharges hand tools at zero fuel cost.

## 7. Match Speed Before Docking
Never approach the Cloud Cruiser's flight deck at maximum thrust. Match the carrier's forward cruising speed (approx. 20 knots) from the lower rear quadrant, then tap `G` to lock magnetic landing clamps once within 15 meters.

## 8. Prioritize Laser Cutter Vibration Tuning
When mining floating sky crystals, hold your vibration laser cutter steady. Rushing the extraction causes mineral fractures, destroying up to 40% of the yield. Wait for the harmonic indicator to turn green before releasing the beam.

## 9. Redirect Shield Power During Lightning Storms
High-altitude squalls generate electromagnetic pulses. On the Cloud Cruiser's power console, route 40% surplus reactor wattage to electrostatic shielding to prevent workbench blowouts and radar blind spots.

## 10. Coordinate 4-Player Co-op Crew Stations
If playing in a co-op squad, assign explicit roles: one Pilot on the carrier helm, one Recon Ace in the Wasp, one Scavenger on the winches, and one Engineer maintaining reactor stability.
