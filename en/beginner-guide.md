## Before your first expedition
ATMOSFAR is available in Early Access, which began October 29, 2026. Use the [Steam purchase and release guide](/release-date/) to check prices and launch content. This guide is preparation advice based on developer descriptions; no launch build has been tested here.

## Check the controls in your installed build
Open the input settings before leaving camp. Find movement, interaction, inventory and the flight actions offered by your current craft. Try them near a safe landing area before taking a longer trip. Follow the displayed bindings if an older guide disagrees; this page does not prescribe unverified glider, grapple or docking shortcuts.

## Give the first trip one objective
Choose one task from your current objective list. Check your inventory, the equipment needed for that task and where you intend to return. Keep a note of what prevented progress so the next trip has a clear purpose. These are planning suggestions, not fixed tutorial steps or measured fuel ranges.

## Use the camp and the right craft
The developer's Wasp overview describes the Utility Wasp as an expedition craft carrying a deployable camp. Check the available camp facilities after landing before planning a refill, crafting task or rest. Compare the [Wasp classes](/guides/wasp-classes-guide/) before assuming every craft has the same equipment.

## Plan the next stage
For longer-term planning, use the [Cloud Cruiser preparation guide](/guides/cloud-cruiser-first-base/). Do not treat a demo boundary or a future roadmap feature as something you must unlock in the launch build. Keep the [expedition checklist](/guides/starter-survival-checklist/) alongside your own notes.
