## Quick answer: where should I look?

**Community answers, checked November 3, 2026:** in the [“cant find silica” thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805391546438500/), players describe large quartz-like crystals in the later islands. Two replies give useful landmarks, but no verified coordinates or complete deposit map.

<details>
<summary>Show Horizon Isles landmarks (location spoilers)</summary>
<p>Rusty Spoons, reply #7, starts at the settlement used for the weather-data objective. Find the nearby trench filled with tires, then look around the tall, narrow rock pillars that flare outward at their tops. The reply places Silica around that area and higher on the sides of the pillars.</p>
<p>The author recalls going south but explicitly expresses uncertainty about that direction. Treat the trench and pillar shapes as the stronger landmarks; this is not a guaranteed compass route.</p>
</details>

## Another player-reported search area

RepairArtist, reply #5 in the same thread, reports a white rock shelf on the upper part of the island with a military post / Cloud Cruiser graveyard. SpinierBuckle61, reply #2, describes personally finding large quartz-like crystals on the last two islands. These are community observations, not proof that every matching island contains a fixed number of nodes.

## Before spending fuel

**Editorial search plan:** compare the island name and active objective, carry return fuel, and inspect the object prompt to confirm that the item is Silica. Record the landmark where you actually collect it. Do not confuse an approximate forum direction with an exact marked deposit.

The older [“silicone?” discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1798230/discussions/0/573805391546275203/) uses ambiguous material wording. Its cave suggestion is a secondary search lead, not a verified Silica route.
