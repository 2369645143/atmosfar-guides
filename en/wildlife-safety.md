## Observe before approaching

Use the field guide above to filter by environment or search English names and English/Chinese behavior notes. Green labels identify visitors described as harmless; limited information does not establish a reliable counter.

## Eight-entry quick reference

| Creature or plant | Identification and behavior | Editorial response tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maratus | Cave-dwelling group predator. Eggs or burrows in dark areas. | Scan the entrance before committing; avoid being surrounded. |
| Bombardier | Territorial; protects its young. Shell withdrawal precedes toxic projectiles. | Back away as it withdraws; do not approach juveniles. |
| Larker | Solitary stalking hunter; poorly documented. Camouflaged, difficult to spot. | Keep a return route; no reliable counter is established here. |
| Batgull | Pursues nearby people and aircraft. An aggressive flying animal nearby. | Give it space and plan an exit before provoking pursuit. |
| Spider Puffin | Harmless, but may steal exposed food. Small visitors around camp or cliffs. | Secure food rather than treating every visitor as an enemy. |
| Barnacle | Island-underside ambusher. Claw or harpoon catches passing targets. | Inspect the underside before flying close to an island. |
| Venus Flytrap | Large trap can enclose a human. An oversized flytrap along your path. | Walk around the trap; no tested escape sequence is provided here. |
| Flame Flower | Proximity triggers a flame burst. A nearby flower starts emitting flame. | Stay outside its reach; avoid pushing through active flames. |

## Three preparation checks

1. Keep track of your route and safe landing areas so you can change course.
2. Check tool charge, repair supplies and flight fuel.
3. Agree on a regroup point and retreat signal when playing co-op.

These are editorial preparation tips. This guide supplies no health, damage, drop-rate, exact attack-range or fixed escape-button claims.
